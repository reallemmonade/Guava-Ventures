Support

Email support@guava.ventures and we will get back to you within two business days. Tell us which app you are using and what you were trying to do.

guava.u

  • My bank won't connect. Try again from Settings. Some banks, like Chase, need an extra redirect step.
  • How do I export or delete my data? My profile › Settings has Export and Erase everything.

Guava Poker

  • I forgot my password. Use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen to get a six-digit code by email.
  • My data isn't on my other device. Sign in with the same account on both. Data saved before you signed in stays on the original device.
  • Delete my account: Account › Delete account.

guava.zip for Mac

  • macOS says Guava can't be opened. Guava isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first open of a downloaded copy is blocked. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, click Open Anyway next to Guava, enter your password, then click Open Anyway again. You only do this once.
  • Totals look low or some folders are skipped. Turn on Full Disk Access: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access, switch Guava on, then quit and reopen it.
  • Turn on the assistant. Add an Anthropic API key in Guava's Settings, or install Ollama to run it on your Mac. The key is stored in your Keychain.
  • Uninstall Guava. Quit it and drag Guava from Applications to the Trash.

Offerday

  • Move my progress to an account. Create a free account on the same device; it offers to bring in the progress saved in your browser.
  • Export or delete my data: Account › Export or Delete account.
