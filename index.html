01 · Consumer · In daily use

guava.u

guava.u keeps your everyday admin in one app on your phone. Today shows how much you can safely spend and what needs you: bills due, renewals, payday, birthdays and who to reach out to. Money covers spending, a budget you can change by chat, bills, transactions and net worth, with read-only bank sync. People and Plans keep friends, gifts and trips in view, and Life holds health, work and home admin like the car and insurance. It installs on an iPhone like an app.

02 · Consumer · Live

Guava Poker

Guava Poker lets you play full hands of Hold'em and Omaha against bots or friends, then study them. A solver runs in your browser to show the best play and how often to take each action, and your mistakes turn into drills, quizzes and hand reviews. Friends can join a private table from their phones, and an account keeps your hands and progress in sync between phone and laptop.

03 · B2B · Prototype

Merqado

Merqado gives a consulting team one place for each engagement: client data, findings, files and the team. Everyone gets a home view that fits their role, from associate to partner. People sign in with their firm's own login, files stay in the client's cloud, and the AI runs on the firm's own model account or on Merqado's, with every call metered so usage can be billed. It is an early prototype.

04 · Careers · Beta

Offerday

Offerday prepares you for private equity, banking and investing interviews. A short placement test sets a daily plan up to your interview date, with drills on the mechanics and the questions you'll be asked. It builds fresh LBO, DCF and merger cases as Excel workbooks with a worked answer key, then grades the model you send back line by line and by formula. Every number comes from a tested solver, not an AI guess. The Tower turns your prep into a pixel-art game: you climb a Wall Street tower floor by floor, and real steps like coffee chats and applications unlock the top.

05 · Mac + iPhone · Beta

guava.zip

guava.zip is a better Finder for the Mac. Next to a fast file browser it finds junk, big forgotten files, duplicates and leftover apps, maps where your space went and lists what runs in the background, and you can move everything a scan finds to the Trash at once. An assistant can find, explain and tidy files with your own Claude key or a model running on the Mac. The iPhone app finds duplicate photos, shrinks big videos and clears out bursts, Live Photos and old screenshots.

06 · AI infrastructure · Live

agent-index

agent-index fetches web pages for AI agents and turns them into clean Markdown with sources, using about 95% fewer tokens than the raw page. One cache is shared by every agent, so a page is fetched once and a repeat read returns only what changed. Agents can search everything already read before fetching anything new, and it all works through MCP tools at guavaindex.com.

07 · Mac + iPhone · In daily use

Celaya

Celaya is a home base for work you hand to AI. It gathers your project threads in one place, flags the ones waiting on you with a one-tap approval or answer, and sends a morning and evening brief. You can hand a task to Claude Code or ChatGPT from it, lay out apps and pages as tiles on a board, and chat with a private model that runs on your own Mac. It works as a Mac app, on iPhone and at celaya.ai.

08 · Marketing · Prototype

QR-Tracker

QR-Tracker shows which flyer and which location bring people in. Each campaign is a grid of places and flyer designs, and every pairing gets its own QR code and short link. Scans are followed through to downloads or sign-ups, and a dashboard shows conversion, cost per sign-up, busy hours and top cities. It exports print-ready flyers with the right code on each page, and teams can share campaigns and connect through an API.
