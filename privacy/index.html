[LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("Guava Ventures", "we") makes guava.u, Guava Poker, Merqado, Offerday and guava.zip for Mac. This policy explains what each product collects, why, and the choices you have. Questions go to privacy@guava.ventures.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your data for third-party advertising.
  • Our apps never move money, place orders or message anyone on your behalf.

guava.u

  • Account: your login and password (stored hashed).
  • Records you enter: spending, bills, plans, people, trips, diary entries and settings.
  • Bank data, if you connect it: read-only balances and transactions through Plaid. Bank access tokens are encrypted and never sent to your browser. See Plaid's end-user privacy policy.
  • Email and contacts, if you connect them: message metadata only (who and when, not message contents), used to suggest who to reach out to.
  • AI features: when you use chat or "log one", the text you send goes to the AI provider you choose in Settings. The app keeps a receipt of every AI request, which you can review in Settings.

Guava Poker

  • On your device: hand histories, drills, saved spots and settings are stored in your browser.
  • If you create an account: your username, email (for password resets) and that same study data sync to our server so they appear on all your devices.
  • Friend games: table state is held in memory only while the table is open.

guava.zip for Mac

Guava runs on your Mac and has no account. It reads your files only to show, search and clean them, and nothing leaves your Mac unless you turn on the assistant. If you add your own Anthropic API key, the questions you ask and the file names and text the assistant looks up are sent to Anthropic under your key to answer you. If you use Ollama instead, everything stays on your Mac. Your key is kept in your Keychain. Reading the text inside files can be switched off in Settings.

Offerday

  • Without an account: your placement results and practice history stay in your browser.
  • If you create an account: your email, an optional name and your password (stored hashed), plus your drill, case and test history so your plan follows you. You can export everything or delete the account from Account.
  • Models you send for grading: used to grade them and show you the report.

Merqado

Merqado is in private development with design partners. Each firm signs in with its own single sign-on, client files stay in the firm's or client's own cloud storage, and AI requests go to the firm's own AI account or Merqado's.
